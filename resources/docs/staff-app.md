## In short

- The app is a web page. Open the link your company gives you (it ends in **/me**) and add it to your phone's home screen. You do not need the Play Store.
- Sign in with your **mobile number** (you get a 6-digit code) or with your **email and password**.
- **Today**: punch in and out (if your company allows it), see today's times, notices and your QR badge for the kiosk.
- **Month**: your attendance for each day, your monthly report as a PDF and, once that month's payroll is final, your payslip.
- **Leave**: see how many leave days you have left and ask for leave.
- **Correction**: tell HR if you forgot to punch, worked outside, or worked from home.

## Open the app and add it to your phone

1. Open the link from HR in your phone's browser. It looks like *yourcompany…/me*.
2. Add it to your home screen, so it opens like an app:
   - **Android (Chrome)**: tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
   - **iPhone (Safari)**: tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Next time, just tap the icon.

## Sign in

You need an account from HR first. If you cannot sign in, ask HR to make one for you.

**With your mobile number** (normal way):

1. Tap **Phone**.
2. Type your **Mobile number** and tap **Send code**.
3. You get a 6-digit code by WhatsApp or SMS. It works for 5 minutes.
4. Type the code and tap **Sign in**.

**With email**: tap **Email**, type your email and **Password**, and tap **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on your phone. To leave, tap **Sign out** at the top.

> **Tip:** Want the app in Urdu? Ask HR. They can change your language to اردو.

## Today

![The Today screen of the staff app, with punch times, the punch button and notices](staff-app-home.jpg)

At the top you see today's date and a short line, for example *"At work since 09:02."* Below are your **In** and **Out** times. A **Late** label shows if you came late.

### Punch in and out from your phone

You see the **Punch in** / **Punch out** button only if your company allows phone punching for you.

1. Be at your workplace (field and remote staff can be anywhere).
2. Tap **Punch in** in the morning, or **Punch out** when you leave.
3. Allow **location** when the phone asks. If the camera opens, look at it: a selfie is taken.
4. You see *"Punched in at 09:02."*

Your location and a selfie are saved with each punch.

If you see *"Saved at 09:02, but … HR will review it."*, your punch was kept but HR must check it first. The message says why: you are far from the office (it shows how far), your branch has no office location set, you are not on the office internet, or your face could not be confirmed. You do not need to punch again.

If you see *"You had already punched a moment ago, so this one was not counted again."*, you (or a machine) punched less than a minute before. Nothing more to do.

More about the rules: [Phone punching](/help/phone-punching).

### Notices

Notices from the company show on this screen. If a notice has an **I have read this** button, read it and tap the button. It then shows *Acknowledged*.

### Optional holidays

If your company has optional holidays (for example a religious festival), they show here. Tap **Take this day** for the ones you want. It then says *Taking it ✓*. Tap again to give it back. On the other optional days you work as normal.

### Kiosk badge

At the bottom is your QR code. Show it to the camera of the kiosk tablet to punch. It changes every day, so do not print it. See [Using the kiosk](/help/using-the-kiosk).

## Month

You see a calendar of the month. Each day has a short code:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Present |
| **L** | Late |
| **HD** | Half day |
| **A** | Absent |
| **MO** | Missing punch-out (you did not punch out) |
| **WO** | Weekly off |
| **H** | Holiday |
| **OD** / **WFH** | Official duty / Work from home |
| **CL**, **SL**, **AL**, **UL** … | Leave (casual, sick, annual, unpaid …) |
| **EX** | Exempted (your company excused this day) |

A **½** after a code means half a day. Tap **What do the codes mean?** in the app to see the list.

- At the top you see how many days you were **Present**, **Late / half** and **Absent**.
- Tap a day to see your **In**, **Out** and **Late** minutes for that day.
- If a day is wrong, tap it and then **Request a correction for this day**.
- Use the arrows to go to the **Previous month** or **Next month**.
- Tap **Download my monthly report (PDF)** to get the same report HR prints.
- When HR has finalised the month's payroll, **Download my payslip (PDF)** appears too (only if your company runs payroll in {app}).

## Leave

At the top you see each leave type and how many **days left** you have.

To ask for leave:

1. Under **Apply for leave**, choose the **Leave type**.
2. Choose **From** and **To** dates.
3. Choose **Duration**: **Full day(s)**, **First half** or **Second half** (half days are for one date only).
4. Write a **Reason** if you like.
5. Add an **Attachment** if needed, for example a medical certificate for sick leave (photo or PDF, up to 5 MB).
6. Tap **Send request**.

Your request shows under **My requests** as *pending*. Your manager and/or HR then approve or reject it, and the status changes to *approved* or *rejected*. Weekly offs and holidays inside your dates are normally not counted.

## Correction

Use this when your attendance is wrong.

1. Choose the **Date**.
2. Under **What happened?** choose one:
   - **I forgot to punch**: give the **Time** and whether it was a punch **In** or **Out**.
   - **Official duty outside the office**: you were working outside for the company.
   - **I worked from home**.
3. Write the **Reason**. This is required.
4. Tap **Send request**.

Your manager will review it. When it is approved, your attendance for that day is fixed.

### Swap a shift

If your company uses rosters, you can also ask a colleague to swap shifts for one day:

1. Under **Swap a shift**, choose the **Date** and the colleague in **Swap with**.
2. Tap **Ask my colleague**.
3. Your colleague taps **Accept** or **Decline** in their app. After they accept, your manager or HR must approve it.

## Common problems

### "Wrong email or password."

Check your email and password. If you forgot the password, ask HR to set a new one, or sign in with your mobile number.

### I did not get the 6-digit code

Check you typed the same mobile number HR has for you. Wait a minute and check WhatsApp and SMS. If it still does not come, ask HR to check your number.

### "That code is wrong or expired."

The code works for 5 minutes only, and only the newest code works. To get a new one, tap **Use another number**, type your number again and tap **Send code**.

### "Too many codes requested. Try again in a few minutes."

You asked for codes too many times. Wait about 10 minutes and try again.

### I don't see the Punch in button

Your company does not allow phone punching, or not for you. Punch on the machine or the kiosk. If you think this is wrong, ask HR.

### "Could not punch. Allow location access and try again."

The app needs your location to punch. Turn on location (GPS) on your phone and allow it for the browser, then try again.

### "The GPS signal is too weak. Move near a window or outside and try again."

Your phone cannot find your place well inside the building. Go near a window or outside and tap the button again.

### "A selfie is required to punch."

Your company needs a photo with each punch. Allow the camera for the browser in your phone's settings and try again.

### "You just punched. Please wait a minute."

Your punch was already saved. Do not tap twice. Wait one minute if you really need to punch again.

### "Not enough balance: 2 day(s) left, 5 requested."

You do not have enough days of that leave type left. Choose fewer days or another leave type, or talk to HR.

### "That month is locked."

HR has closed that month, so it cannot be changed from the app. Talk to HR.
