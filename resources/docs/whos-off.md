## In short

- **Who's off** shows one week, Monday to Sunday, one card per day.
- Each card lists who is away that day: approved leave, official duty or work from home, holidays and weekly off days.
- People who have left are not shown after their leaving date.
- Only **approved** leave shows. Pending requests are not on it.
- Filter by **Branch** and **Department**. Use the arrows to move week by week.
- HR and the Owner see the whole company. A manager sees only their team and themselves.

## What you see on this page

![Who's off for one week, with leave and holidays on each day](whos-off-week.jpg)

At the top:

- **Week** — the arrows move one week back or forward.
- **All branches** — choose one branch (shown only if you have more than one).
- **All departments** — choose one department.
- *Showing your team.* — appears if you are a manager and see only your own people.
- The colour key.

Each day card shows today with a coloured border. Each line is a name and a coloured label:

| Colour | Label | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow | The leave type, for example *Annual leave*, or *Annual leave (half day)* | Approved leave on that day. |
| Blue | *Official duty* or *Work from home* | The employee is working, but not at the office. |
| Green | *Holiday*, or *Holiday, Lahore only* | A holiday. The line shows the holiday's name. A branch holiday names the branch. |
| Green | *Optional holiday: Eid Milad* | The employee is taking this optional holiday. |
| Grey | *Off day* | The employee's weekly off, from their shift (or their branch). When everyone shown has the day off, one line says **Everyone**. |

A day with nobody away shows **Everyone in**.

## How to use it

- **Before you approve leave**, check who else is off the same days in that department.
- **Planning the week**, check who is away before you change the [Roster](/help/roster).
- **Before a holiday**, check that branch holidays show for the right branch.

The page only shows information. To change leave, go to [Leave requests](/help/leave-requests). To change holidays, go to [Holidays](/help/holidays).

## Common problems

### Someone on leave is not shown

Their request is still pending, or was rejected or cancelled. Only approved leave shows. Check [Leave requests](/help/leave-requests).

### Someone shows "Off day" on the wrong day

The off day comes from the employee's shift on that date, or from their branch when the shift has no weekly offs. Check the shift under [Shifts](/help/shifts) and the branch's weekly offs.

### As a manager I see only a few people

Managers see only the employees who report to them (set as **Manager** on the employee's profile) and themselves. HR and the Owner see everyone.

### A holiday for another branch shows

With **All branches**, holidays for every branch are shown, each with its branch name. Choose a branch to see only that branch's and company-wide holidays.

### I can't see Who's off in the menu

Your role cannot request leave (for example Reception). Ask the Owner or HR to change your role; see [Users and roles](/help/users-and-roles).
