## In short

- The kiosk is a tablet at the gate or office door. You punch on it when you come and when you go.
- You can punch with your **PIN** (your employee number on the machine), your **QR** badge from the staff app, or your **Face**. Your company decides which of these the kiosk shows.
- When it works, the screen turns green and shows your name, the time and **IN** or **OUT**.
- Punch **once**. If you punch again within a minute, nothing new is saved.
- If the screen shows a notice after your punch, read it and tap the button.
- Forgot your PIN? Ask HR.

## Punch with your PIN

Your PIN is your number on the attendance machine. HR gives it to you.

1. Make sure **PIN** is chosen at the top (if you see **PIN**, **QR** and **Face** buttons).
2. Type your PIN on the number keys. Tap **⌫** to delete a wrong number.
3. Under the keys, leave **Auto** chosen. The kiosk knows if you are coming in or going out. Choose **Punch in** or **Punch out** only if it gets it wrong.
4. Tap the **✓** key.
5. If the screen says *"Now enter your 4-digit kiosk PIN"*, type your secret 4-digit kiosk PIN. It is sent by itself after the 4th number.

Some kiosks take a photo when you punch. Look at the camera.

## Punch with your QR badge

1. Open the staff app on your phone (see [Staff app](/help/staff-app)). Your QR code is under **Kiosk badge** on the **Today** screen.
2. On the kiosk, tap **QR**. The screen says *Show your QR badge*.
3. Hold your phone up to the tablet's camera, with the QR code inside the frame.

The QR code changes every day. A screenshot from yesterday will not work; open the app each time.

## Punch with your face

Your face must be enrolled by HR first (they do it once with a camera).

1. Tap **Face**.
2. Stand alone in front of the tablet and look at the camera.
3. When it says *Now turn your head*, turn your head slowly to the left and to the right.
4. Wait for *Checking…*. The kiosk finds you and saves the punch.

If your face is not found, tap **Use PIN instead**.

> **Tip:** Remove sunglasses, caps and masks. Stand in front of the tablet, not to the side.

## What you see after punching

| Screen | What it means |
|---|---|
| Green, *"Good morning, Ali"*, the time and **IN** | Your punch in is saved. |
| Green, *"Goodbye, Ali"*, the time and **OUT** | Your punch out is saved. |
| *Saved. HR will check this punch.* | Your punch is kept, but HR must check it (for example the photo did not match your face). You do not need to punch again. |
| *Offline. It will sync automatically.* | The internet is down. Your punch is kept on the tablet and sent later. You do not need to punch again. |
| *"Ali, you already punched"* and *Nothing new was saved. Wait a minute before punching again.* | You punched a moment ago. Your first punch counts. |
| Red, with a message and *Please try again.* | The punch was not saved. Read the message (see below). |

### Notices

After your punch, the kiosk may show a **Notice · اطلاع** from the company. Read it.

- If the button says **I have read it / میں نے پڑھ لیا**, tap it. This tells HR you have read the notice.
- If the button says **OK**, tap it to close.

If there are more notices, you see *(1 / 2)*; tap the button to see the next one.

## Common problems

### "Not recognised. Please try again."

The PIN is wrong, or the kiosk PIN is wrong, or your QR code is old. Type your PIN again carefully. For QR, open the staff app again to get today's code. If it still fails, ask HR to check your PIN.

### "Please use the kiosk at your own branch."

This kiosk is for another branch. Punch on the kiosk at the branch where you work, or ask HR to check your branch.

### "Face not recognised. Please use your PIN."

The kiosk could not match your face. Tap **Use PIN instead** and punch with your PIN. If this happens often, ask HR to enrol your face again.

### "Face not captured. Look at the camera and try again."

The camera did not see your face clearly. Stand straight in front of the tablet, in good light, and try again.

### "No connection. Please use your PIN."

The internet is down, and face punching needs the internet. Use your PIN or QR badge; those are saved on the tablet and sent later.

### The screen shows a lock and "This screen opens by itself once the tablet is approved."

This tablet is not approved yet. You cannot punch on it. Tell HR or reception; they must approve the tablet.

### I punched but my attendance in the app does not show it

Punches can take a moment to show. If the kiosk was offline, the punch is sent when the internet is back. If it still does not show after some time, ask for a correction in the staff app (**Correction → I forgot to punch**).
