## In short

- This page appears only if your plan includes the **Visitor log**.
- Reception clicks **Check in visitor**, types the name and whom they are visiting, and can take a photo. {app} gives each visitor a badge number: V001, V002 and so on, starting again every day.
- **Print badge** prints a small visitor card. **Check out** records when the visitor leaves.
- **Edit** fixes a visitor's details. HR, the owner and admins can also **Delete** an entry made by mistake.
- The list opens on **today's** visitors. **Still inside** shows who has not checked out.
- Visitors are not employees: they do not affect attendance or payroll.

## What you see on this page

![Visitors list with today's visitors, their badge numbers and in/out times](visitors.jpg)

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Badge** | The badge number for the day, for example V003. |
| **Name** | The visitor's name. Use the search box to find a name. |
| **Company** | The company the visitor comes from. |
| **Visiting** | The employee they came to see. |
| **Purpose** | Why they came. |
| **In** | Date and time of check-in. |
| **Out** | Time of check-out, or *inside* if they have not left yet. |

Filters:

- **Today** is on at first. Turn it off to see earlier visitors.
- **Still inside** shows only visitors who have not checked out, from any day.

## Who can use it

Reception (the Receptionist role), HR, the owner and admins. Only HR, the owner and admins can delete an entry.

## Check in a visitor

1. Click **Check in visitor** at the top right.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Name** (required),
   - **Phone**,
   - **CNIC**: 13 digits without dashes, optional,
   - **Company**,
   - **Visiting**: pick the employee they came to see,
   - **Purpose**, for example *Interview* or *Delivery*,
   - **Branch**, if you have more than one,
   - **Photo**: take or upload a picture of the visitor (up to 2 MB).
3. Click **Create**.

![The Check in visitor form](visitors-check-in.jpg)

The check-in time is set to now, and the next badge number of the day is given.

## Print a badge

Click **Print badge** on the visitor's row. A card opens in a new tab and the print window opens by itself. The card shows your company name, the word *VISITOR*, the photo, name, company, whom they are visiting, the check-in time and the badge number. It is the size of an ID card (86 × 54 mm).

> **Tip:** No card printer? Print on plain paper and cut it out, or put it in a plastic badge holder.

## Check out a visitor

When the visitor leaves, click **Check out** on their row. The **Out** column shows the time. The button disappears once the visitor is checked out.

> **Tip:** Before closing reception, tick **Still inside** to see anyone who left without checking out.

## Fix or delete an entry

- **Edit** on a visitor's row opens the check-in details. Correct them and click **Save changes**. The badge number and the check-in time stay the same.
- **Delete** (HR, owner and admins) removes an entry made by mistake, with its photo. This cannot be undone. The other visitors keep their badge numbers.

## Visitor report

For a list over a longer time, use the **Visitor log** report (see [Other reports](/help/other-reports)). You can export it to Excel or PDF. It needs full report access (Owner, Admin, HR, Branch manager), so a Receptionist does not see it.

## Common problems

### I can't see Visitors in the menu

Your plan does not include the Visitor log, or your role cannot use it. Reception, HR, the owner and admins see this page. To add the Visitor log to your plan, see [Billing](/help/billing).

### "The CNIC field format is invalid."

Type the CNIC as 13 digits with no dashes or spaces, for example 3520212345671. You can also leave it empty.

### I made a mistake in a visitor's details

Click **Edit** on the visitor's row and correct it. If the whole entry was a mistake, ask HR or an admin to **Delete** it.

### The badge has no photo

No photo was added at check-in. The badge prints without it.

### The print window did not open

Your browser may have blocked it. Press Ctrl + P on the badge tab to print.
