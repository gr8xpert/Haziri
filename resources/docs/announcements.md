## In short

- An announcement is a notice for your staff, for example "Office closed on 14 Aug 2026".
- Staff see it in the staff app (**/me**) and on the kiosk tablet after they punch. You can also send it by email, WhatsApp or SMS, and print it for the notice board.
- Send it to everyone, or only to some **Branches** or **Departments**.
- Turn on **Employees must acknowledge** to make staff tap "I have read this". The **Acknowledged** column counts them.
- Set **Publish at** to send it later, and **Expires at** to hide it after a date.
- Owner, Admin and HR can make announcements.

## What you see on this page

![The Announcements list with title, dates, Ack and Acknowledged columns](announcements.jpg)

The newest announcement is on top.

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The heading of the notice. |
| **Publish at** | When staff start to see it. *immediately* means at once. |
| **Expires at** | When it disappears from the app and kiosk. *never* means it stays. |
| **Ack** | A tick means staff must acknowledge it. |
| **Acknowledged** | How many employees have tapped "I have read this" (in the app or on the kiosk). |

Each row has **Print**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

## How to send an announcement

1. Click **New announcement** at the top right.
2. Write the **Title** and the **Body** (the message). You can write in Urdu.
3. Choose who it is for:
   - **Branches**: leave it on *All branches*, or pick one or more.
   - **Departments**: leave it on *All departments*, or pick one or more.
   If you pick both, only people in those branches **and** those departments get it.
4. **Publish at**: leave it blank to send now, or pick a date and time to send later.
5. **Expires at**: optional. After this time the notice is hidden.
6. Turn on **Employees must acknowledge** if every person must confirm they read it.
7. Keep **Send a notification** on to tell people, and tick how under **Send by**: **In the app**, **Email**, and **WhatsApp** or **SMS** if your company has them set up.
8. Click **Create**.

![The New announcement form](announcement-new.jpg)

Under **Send by** you see how many people you will reach, for example: *"312 employees: 40 have the app, 25 an email address, 290 a phone number (for WhatsApp / SMS)."* Use this to choose the channels. People without the app still see the notice on the kiosk after punching.

> **Tip:** Factory workers often have no app or email. Turn on **SMS** or **WhatsApp**, and use **Print** for the notice board.

## Who sees it, and where

- **Staff app (/me)**: on the **Today** screen, the latest 5 announcements that are live. If acknowledgement is needed, there is an **I have read this** button; after tapping, it shows *Acknowledged*.
- **Kiosk tablet**: after a punch, the person sees up to 3 notices: ones they still have to acknowledge, and other notices from the last 2 days. The button says *I have read it / میں نے پڑھ لیا* or *OK*. See [Using the kiosk](/help/using-the-kiosk).
- **Email, WhatsApp, SMS**: sent once, when the announcement is published. A notice with a later **Publish at** is sent at that time. Delivery shows under [Notifications](/help/notifications).
- **Print**: opens a page ready to print for the notice board.

People who have left the company do not receive it.

## Change or remove an announcement

- **Edit** changes the text, audience or dates. The notification is **not** sent again, even if you change the text. To tell people again, make a new announcement.
- **Delete** removes it from the app and the kiosk.
- To stop showing it but keep it in the list, set **Expires at** to now.

## Common problems

### Some staff did not get the WhatsApp or SMS

They have no cell phone on their employee profile, or the number is wrong. Add it under [Employees](/help/employees) (**More details → Contact → Cell phone**). Check **System → Notification log** for failed messages.

### WhatsApp or SMS is not in the Send by list

Your company has not set up a WhatsApp or SMS provider, or your plan does not include WhatsApp alerts. See [Notifications](/help/notifications).

### An employee says they cannot see the announcement

Check the **Branches** and **Departments** you chose, and the branch and department on the employee's profile. Also check **Publish at** has passed and **Expires at** has not.

### I changed the text but nobody was told

Editing never sends the notification again. Make a new announcement with the corrected text, and delete the old one.

### The Acknowledged number stays low

Only staff who open the app or punch on a kiosk can acknowledge. Ask managers to remind their team, or print the notice for the notice board.
